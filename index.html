I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign, advised by Partha S. Dey. My research interests lie in probability theory, especially Stein’s method, random graphs, and minimal spanning trees.

I received my Master’s in Mathematics from Illinois in 2024 and my Bachelor’s in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Chaitanya Bharathi Institute of Technology, Hyderabad, in 2019. Before graduate school, I worked as a Power Integrity sign-off engineer at Soctronics Technologies, Hyderabad (2019–2021).
